Hand Cricket Rules

🔥 Crazy Mode (0-10):

  • Numbers: Throw any number between 0 and 10.
  • Successor / Predecessor = OUT: If bowler throws $N-1$ or $N+1$ of batter's throw, the batter is OUT! (e.g. Batter 5 vs Bowler 4 or 6 = OUT!).
  • Same Number = Multiplier Slam: If both throw the exact same number, runs are multiplied and added! (e.g. 6 and 6 = +36 Runs!).
  • Different Numbers: Batter adds their thrown number to total score.

🏏 Normal Mode (1-6):

  • Numbers: Classic range 1 to 6.
  • Same Number = OUT: If both throw the same number, batter is OUT!
  • Different Numbers: Batter scores their chosen number.
⚡ Zero Countdown: As soon as both players lock in their hands, the ball is immediately bowled with pure CSS animations!

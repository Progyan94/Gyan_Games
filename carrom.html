Carrom Board Rules

⚪ White Pieces (10 pts): Assigned to Player 1.

⚫ Black Pieces (5 pts): Assigned to Player 2 / Bot.

🔴 The Queen (25 pts): Can be pocketed anytime after at least 1 coin is potted. It must be covered by pocketing another coin on the same or next turn.

🎯 Streak Turn: If you pocket your coin, you get to strike again!

⚠️ Striker Foul: Pocketing your striker incurs a 1-coin penalty returned to the center, and your turn ends.
